Common topics

  • Data location — Your content is stored on your device. If you connect another device using Sync, a shared copy is stored in Firebase.
  • Restore purchase — Use your Apple ID or Google account purchase history to reinstall Life on a new device. To move content, use optional Sync with your existing device or restore a device backup that includes Life data. Reinstalling the app alone does not restore cloud content without the original sync identity.
  • Bugs — Please include your OS version, device model, and a short description of what happened.

Delete a sync account or request data deletion

In Life 1.4 or later, open Budget → Sync → Delete sync account and confirm. Stay online until deletion completes. This removes the anonymous Firebase account, pairing address, and its shared cloud rooms. Local content and copies already held on another device remain on those devices. Stop syncing disconnects devices and removes the current shared room without deleting the anonymous account.

If you cannot use the app, email k.rise.media@icloud.com with a Life data deletion request. Tell us which installation you want help with and its sync code if available. Do not include passwords, private notes, or financial records. We will explain any verification needed before deleting data. Provider backups and security logs may have separate retention periods, as described in the privacy policy.
