Life · Command Center (“Life”, “the App”) is a personal productivity app for tasks, notes, calendar, workouts, and simple money tracking, published by K.Rise.Media LLC. This policy explains what data the App handles and how.

1. Local use and optional Sync

Life stores your tasks, notes, calendar events, workout plans and completion history, and finance entries on your device. Core features work without creating a personal sign-in account. If you choose to connect another device using Sync, Life uploads a shared copy of this content to Google Firebase so approved devices can exchange changes.

In Life 1.4 and later, an anonymous Firebase account and a six-character sync code are created when you first open Sync. Earlier versions may create this technical account when the app launches. The account does not require your name, email address, or password. Its persistent identifier still identifies the app installation and is used to control access to shared data.

2. Information handled

  • Your content: task and note text; calendar titles, dates, and any locations or notes you type; workout plans and completion history; transaction amounts, dates, categories, budgets, recurring templates, and starting balances. You decide what to enter.
  • Sync information: anonymous account identifiers, sync codes, approved device membership, connection requests, and synchronization revisions.
  • Technical information: Firebase and its infrastructure process information needed to deliver and secure requests, which can include IP addresses and service logs. Apple and Google may also provide platform diagnostics according to their own settings and policies.
  • Support: if you email us, we receive the information you choose to include, such as your email address and description of the issue.

Life does not connect to your bank, read your system calendar or contacts, or collect your device location for these features. A location typed into a calendar event is part of the content you enter.

3. How information is used and shared

We use this information to provide local app features, synchronize approved devices, control access, resolve synchronization conflicts, and respond to support or deletion requests. When two devices change the same record, Life can retain the alternative version in a note for your review.

Sync content is shared with the device you approve and processed by Google Firebase on our behalf. The person with access to that device can see and retain the synced content. Only approve devices you trust. Connecting for the first time gives the requesting device's data precedence, as explained in the connection prompt.

We do not sell your personal information, use your Life content for advertising profiling, or include third-party advertising or behavioral analytics SDKs. Information may also be disclosed when required by law or necessary to protect rights and security.

4. Storage and security

Local content is stored in the app's on-device storage and is protected by the device's operating-system security. Optional Sync uses Firebase Authentication and Cloud Firestore. Network connections use encryption in transit, and Google provides encryption at rest for Firestore. Sync is not end-to-end encrypted: authorized cloud service processing can access its contents. No storage or transmission method can be guaranteed completely secure.

Device backups, exports you create, and platform diagnostics are subject to your device settings and the relevant service provider's policies. See Firebase privacy and security information.

5. Retention and deletion

Local data remains until you remove it, clear app storage, or remove the app. Operating-system backups may retain copies independently of the installed app. Exported files and copies on another device are not removed by deleting this app.

While devices are linked, Firestore retains the shared content needed for Sync. Stop syncing removes the shared cloud room and disconnects the devices after the request completes online; data already stored on either device remains there.

In Life 1.4 and later, open Today → Sync → Delete sync account to remove this installation's pairing address, shared cloud rooms, and anonymous Firebase account. Stay online until deletion completes. The app retains local content and can create a new sync account if you later reopen Sync. Deleting the app alone does not request deletion of its cloud account or shared content.

If you cannot access the app, or use an older version, contact k.rise.media@icloud.com for help. Do not email your private notes or financial records. We may ask for information needed to verify control of the relevant installation before acting. Provider security logs and backups may be retained separately under applicable service retention policies or legal requirements.

6. Purchases and permissions

Apple or Google processes store purchases. We do not receive full payment-card details. Core Life features do not require broad device permissions. When you choose Export, the operating-system share sheet lets you select the destination; that destination's privacy practices apply.

7. Your choices

You can use Life locally, choose whether to connect another device, stop syncing, delete the sync account, edit or remove content, and control device backups. You can contact us about access, correction, deletion, or other privacy rights available under the law where you live.

8. Children

Life is not directed at children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information through Life, contact us so we can investigate and take appropriate deletion steps.

9. International processing

K.Rise.Media LLC operates in the United States. Optional cloud synchronization and support may involve processing outside your country, including through Google's infrastructure. Applicable service-provider safeguards and privacy terms govern that processing.

10. Changes

We may update this policy when app behavior or service practices change. The date above identifies the latest revision.
