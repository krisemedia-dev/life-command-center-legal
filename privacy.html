Privacy Policy

Life · Command Center
Effective: July 21, 2026
Contact: k.rise.media@icloud.com

Life · Command Center (“Life”, “the App”) is a personal productivity app for tasks, notes, calendar, workouts, and simple money tracking. This policy explains what data the App handles and how.

1. Summary

Life is designed to keep your data on your device. We do not operate a Life user account system, and we do not upload your tasks, notes, calendar events, workouts, or finance entries to our servers as part of the App’s normal operation.

2. Information the App stores

Content you enter in the App (for example tasks, notes, events, workout plans, categories, and transactions) is stored locally on your device using on-device storage provided by the operating system.

The App does not require you to create an account or sign in with us to use core features.

3. Information we do not collect

  • We do not collect your task lists, notes, calendar details, workout logs, or financial entries for our own analytics databases.
  • We do not sell personal information.
  • We do not use your Life content for advertising profiling.

4. Purchases

If you purchase Life through the Apple App Store, payment is processed by Apple. We do not receive or store your full payment card details. Apple may share limited purchase status with us as needed to unlock the app under Apple’s standard commerce rules.

5. Device permissions & system services

Life is built to work without broad device permission prompts for core local features. If future versions request optional permissions (for example notifications or calendar access), those will be used only for the stated feature and can be controlled in system Settings.

6. Crash & platform diagnostics

Apple may collect standard App Store / OS diagnostics (such as crash logs) according to your device settings and Apple’s privacy policy. That data is controlled by Apple, not by a Life backend operated by us.

7. Children

Life is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

8. Data retention & deletion

Because data is stored on your device, you can remove it by deleting the App or clearing App data via system tools. Uninstalling Life removes local Life data from that device.

9. International users

The App is available through the App Store in territories you select at purchase. Processing related to App Store distribution follows Apple’s policies for your region.

10. Changes

We may update this policy as the App evolves. Material changes will be reflected by updating the effective date on this page.

11. Contact

Questions about privacy: k.rise.media@icloud.com
